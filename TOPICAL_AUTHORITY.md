# Topical Authority — toilet.portabel.id

## Role and boundary

Toilet.portabel.id is a specialist education and commercial-support property for portable sanitation units sold or rented in Indonesia. It should help event organizers, contractors, facility teams, emergency planners, and procurement staff choose, size, transport, set up, service, inspect, and return units. Existing product and location pages retain transactional intent. Unit types, materials, capacity, waste/water systems, service frequency, lifting, accessibility, hygiene, permits, standards, certification, warranty, rental terms, and prices require current project- and supplier-specific evidence.

## Context used

- The refreshed repository contains 1,702 unique sitemap paths across 12 sitemap XML files.
- Generated geography families dominate the inventory: 490 fiberglass, 490 HDPE, 491 PVC, and 102 legacy `jual-sewa-<place>` paths.
- Meaningful product hubs include fiberglass types A/B/C, HDPE models, PVC single/double/triple/couple/emergency/premium/standard/urinal variants, and the remaining legacy type pages.
- The refreshed homepage identifies a portable-toilet sale/rental service and separates fiberglass, HDPE, and PVC product families.

## Ignored template noise

- The 1,573 material/location and legacy sale/rental swaps are transactional templates, not distinct editorial subjects.
- Author, category, archive, feed, verification, WordPress, and unrelated `/downloads/` starter-template routes do not count as topical coverage.
- Repeated metadata, superlatives, and price/tier labels such as premium, standard, or low-price were not treated as evidence of specification or value.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub and the next operational decision. Fundamentals link to unit types and quantity planning; type pages link to waste/water systems; quantity pages link to event or worksite use; site setup links to transport, accessibility, and servicing; troubleshooting links to cleaning, maintenance, replacement, or rental/warranty terms. Commercial pages receive links only after the reader has defined unit, quantity, site, and service scope.
